## Scenes

Walkthrough:

| # | Sec | Screen | Action | Caption |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3.5 | Intro card | - | Still copying numbers by hand? |
| 2 | 6 | Task list | type "invoice" in search | Type a word to **filter the list** |

Motion promo (add the Motion column and fill it with different kinds of movement):

| # | Sec | Screen | Motion | Caption |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 | White | words sharpen in one by one | - |
| 2 | 5 | Task list | circle reveal; **wide first**, then zoom to search; typed text | Type a word to **filter the list** |
| 3 | 4 | Task detail | wide first, then zoom to panel; cursor clicks Done | Everything **in one panel** |
| 4 | 4 | Summary | "Task completed" chip flies from the button into the counter; 7 -> 8 | Finish it and **the count updates** |
| 5 | 3 | Header | zoom to Export; file icon pops out and grows | Export **any time** |
| 6 | 3 | White | closing line sharpens in | - |

Motion vocabulary to choose from: zoom in / pull back, pan, typed text, cursor click with ripple, chip or file travelling between screens, screen shrinking away to reveal the next, number count-up, progress dots filling, burst on success, word reveal, wipe-in caption. If two neighbouring scenes use the same movement, change one.

## Intro and ending

- Intro: one line, the viewer's problem, ending with a question mark. Taken from real user questions where possible
- Ending: one line with the outcome, plus the logo. Keep one closing line per video
